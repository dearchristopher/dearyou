# dearyoutx.com home — two concepts (v5)

2026-10-07 · site-designer · for Chris

Lessons from a/b/c/mock: they were *sections*. b had the right silence but posed like a museum. The fix is not more sections, it's one idea that every screen obeys. Both concepts below use only existing assets (band photo, logo, hand lettering, Fault Line cover, horns, drip, sitting person) plus flat shapes. Neither shows the LP title, date, or cover.

All copy is **draft** until the band rewrites it.

---

## A · "someone's in the room"  ← built

**Idea.** The whole site is one empty room, and you're in it. A single ink line, the floor, runs from the first screen to the last, and it never leaves. The crouching guy sits on it, feet hanging over the edge, and he's with you the whole way down. Each scroll stop changes what's in the room with him: the band, the new song, the next thing that isn't ready, the horns coming up behind the floor. He never visibly walks. He just *isn't where you left him* when you look back. At the bottom, something pale is sitting next to him that wasn't there before. That's the record without saying the record: a person, a devil, a ghost, one room. Quiet, a little funny, a little sad, and the mystery pays off in shapes rather than words.

**Storyboard**

| # | Viewport | What moves | Transition out |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Full-bleed band photo, huge bone handwritten logo bottom-left. | Pinned. On scroll the photo scales down into a "window" on a bone wall; logo lifts away; the floor line draws in from the left. | Guy is just *there* on the floor when the line finishes. |
| 2 | Bone room. Giant lowercase blurb fills the top two-thirds, floor + guy below. | Pinned. Words fill from ghost-grey to ink as you scroll (scroll-scrubbed). | Guy is somewhere else on the floor. |
| 3 | Small Fault Line cover leaning on the floor like a record against a wall. | Pinned. Cover scales to full-bleed and swallows the room (guy and floor gone). Ink bar slides up: hand-lettered OUT NOW + 4 listen links. | Cover goes dark. |
| 4 | Lights off: full ink. "next." huge in bone, "not yet." small. | Pinned. The rust drip lowers from the ceiling, scroll-scrubbed. Floor is back as a bone line; the guy is bone now, like a negative. | Crossfade back to bone. |
| 5 | Bone room, guy far left on the floor, empty right side. | Pinned. Horns rise from behind the floor line, slow. Stop scrolling and they sink back. Poke them 3× and they drop and a typewriter date appears under the floor. | Floor stays, guy leaves. |
| 6 | Giant stacked links: bandcamp (rust, helps most), spotify, apple music, youtube. Then instagram, tiktok, facebook, x. | Staggered rise-in, line by line. | Normal scroll. |
| 7 | One big rust circle, hand-lettered MERCH inside. | Scales up from a dot as it enters. | Normal scroll. |
| 8 | Floor line, the guy, horizontal logo bled edge to edge, YOU FOUND THE BOTTOM. | Wait a second at the bottom and a pale sheet-shape fades in beside him. | End. |

## B · "are you still there?"

**Idea.** The page is one side of a phone call made ten years late. Every screen is a single line from the band, set enormous and lowercase, full-bleed, alternating bone / ink / rust panels like the cover of Fault Line split down the middle. "hello?" "is this still your number?" "we made something." The fan never speaks; the content is the reply. Dear You is a letter salutation, so the whole site reads like a letter you scroll. The ghost is the band; the fan is the one who kept the line open.

**Storyboard**

| # | Viewport | What moves | Transition out |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Full-bleed band photo, logo, one word: "hello?" | Photo slowly zooms in on scroll (scale 1→1.15). | Hard cut to ink. |
| 2 | Ink. "it's been a while." | Words stagger in, one per beat. | Panel wipes up. |
| 3 | Bone. Blurb as the next line of the call. | Word scrub. | Split: panel halves slide apart (Fault Line's vertical cut). |
| 4 | Fault Line cover full-bleed between the halves. "we made something." | Cover scales in from the seam. | Halves close. |
| 5 | Ink. "the next one isn't ready. sorry." Drip hangs from the top. | Drip lowers. | Crossfade. |
| 6 | Bone. "...who else is on this line?" Horns peek over a rule. | Horns rise; 3 clicks = date. | Cut. |
| 7 | Listen + socials as "where to find us." | Stagger. | — |
| 8 | Footer: logo, "you found the bottom." | — | End. |

## Pick: A

- **One object, one line.** A's through-line is literal: the floor never leaves the screen, so nothing can read as "a random section." B's through-line is copy, and copy is the thing the band hasn't written yet. If the lines are weak, B collapses into slides.
- **Built on what Chris loved.** The guy sitting on the rule and the horns over the rule were the two strongest reactions in five rounds. A turns them into the spine. B uses them as cameos.
- **Weird the right way.** In A the oddity comes from timing and placement: he moves when you're not looking, the horns sink when you stare, the ghost only shows up for people who stay. B's oddity is mostly words, which leans on copywriting rather than craft.
- **Risk in A:** a fixed floor layer could fight content for the bottom of the screen. Managed by giving every pinned scene the same floor height and letting the guy leave during the flowing sections.
