## Takeaways for Dear You
- Genre norm: site = merch store + tour dates. Almost no prose. Dear You has no tour → can't win that layout; differentiate on the letter/story.
- Best pattern to steal: Sweet Pill — one photo/art, one headline, one kicker line, max 2 buttons.
- Macseal's contact block (general / mgmt / booking / merch) is the standard footer.
- Nobody has a real email signup above the fold except as a popup. A visible inline signup is a gap Dear You can own.
- Copy voice in the scene: lowercase, deadpan, short. Long-form letters are rare → the 2025 return letter is a genuine differentiator.
